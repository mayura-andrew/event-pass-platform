Workshops are sessions inside an event, each with a time, room and optional capacity. The same pass works at all of them.

Add a workshop

On the event's Workshops tab, choose Add workshop and pick who can attend:

  • Everyone at the event: no sign-up needed.
  • Participants sign up: participants sign up on their pass page. With a capacity and the waitlist option, extra people wait and move up automatically when someone cancels.
  • Only people organizers add: invitation only.

People can't sign up for two workshops at the same time.

Attendance

Each workshop gets its own checkpoint for attendance. At the door, choose it on the scanner. Someone who needed to sign up and didn't shows amber, Not signed up; staff can add them on the spot with Add to workshop if there's room.

The Workshops tab shows signed up, waiting and attended for each workshop; so does the Dashboard.
