Any phone with a browser is a scanner: no app to install. Open the event and choose Scan.

  1. Pick the Checkpoint at the top.
  2. Choose Start camera and allow the camera when the browser asks. Point it at the QR code; there's no button to press.
  3. With Fast lane: record on scan ticked, a valid pass is recorded straight away. Untick it to check first and press Record yourself.

Tips

  • Camera won't start? Check the browser's site permissions for the camera, and that the address starts with https://. Close other apps using the camera.
  • Code won't read? Turn the screen brightness up on the participant's phone, or hold the pass a little further away. A printed PDF should be printed at actual size (100%).
  • No camera at all? Use Type or paste a code, or Find someone to search by name or pass ID.
  • The phone buzzes once for green and twice for anything else, so you can keep your eyes on the line.

Without a connection

If the connection drops, scans you record are kept on the phone and a note shows how many are waiting. They're sent automatically when the connection is back. They're kept even if the browser is closed: open Scan again on the same phone and browser to send them. Don't clear the browser's data until the note is gone.
