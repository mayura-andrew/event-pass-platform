Everything about who can register is on the event's Registration tab.

Questions

Everyone is asked for their full name and email. Add other questions with Add question: short or long text, phone, number, date, yes/no, choose one or several from options, consent, and more. For each question choose who sees the answer:

  • Organizers only: for things like phone numbers.
  • Organizers and check-in staff: shown on the scanner, for example a food preference at the meal checkpoint.
  • Also the participant: shown on their pass page.
  • Printed on the pass: drawn on the pass image itself, such as a team name.

Birth dates and under-18s

To know who is under 18, add a question of the type Birth date (under-18 check) (one per event). Then set Age is counted on in the registration settings, for example 14 November: anyone who hasn't turned 18 by that day is marked Under 18. Without a date, the event's start date is used.

Organizers see an Under 18 filter on the Participants tab and a count on the Dashboard. Door staff see an Under 18 badge on the scanner, even if the birth date itself is visible to organizers only. A connected Google Sheet gets an Under 18 column.

Who gets a pass

  • Everyone who registers gets their pass straight away.
  • Only people you approve wait as pending until you choose Approve on the Participants tab. They get their pass then.

Set a Capacity in the event's Settings to limit places. Tick the waitlist option to put people on a waitlist when it's full; Give a place on the Participants tab lets someone in. Opens and Closes limit when the form accepts registrations.

Publishing and visibility

A draft is visible only to your team. Publish puts it online. What that means depends on the event's visibility, set in Settings:

  • Public: listed on your organization's page, with a registration form.
  • Unlisted: a registration form for anyone with the link. Good for invitation-only events where you send the link yourself.
  • Private: no public page or form. Add people by importing a Google Form.

Someone who registers twice with the same email isn't added twice: they get their existing pass again.
