Already collecting sign-ups in a Google Form? Connect it and every response becomes a participant with a pass, checked about once a minute.

  1. On the event's Integrations tab, choose Connect a Google Form and allow access with the Google account that owns the form. The app can read forms and responses; it changes nothing.
  2. Paste the form's link from the form editor's address bar (it contains /forms/d/), then Link form. The public link that people fill in won't work here.
  3. Match the questions: which one is the full name, which the email (or use the email Google collects), and where other answers go, for example Dietary needs into your event's food question.
  4. Choose Start importing.

What happens to each response

  • Each response is imported once, however often the form is checked.
  • Someone already registered with the same email is skipped, not added twice.
  • A response without a usable name or email isn't imported; the tab says how many.
  • Answers that don't match your question's options are left out; the person is still imported.
  • Imported people get their pass by email, the same as people who used your registration page. Capacity, approval and the waitlist apply too.

Import now checks for new responses immediately.
