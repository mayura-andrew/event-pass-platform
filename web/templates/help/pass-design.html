Designs are shared across your organization's events. Each event uses one design; the default is the Classic pass, with a big colored status band, the name and the QR code.

Make a design

  1. Open Pass templates from your organization's tabs and start from a gallery design: Classic, Conference badge (a 4 × 3 inch badge for badge printers) or Minimal.
  2. In the designer, drag elements to move them and use the corner handle to resize. Arrow keys nudge the selected element; Delete removes it; undo and redo work as usual.
  3. Text can include details such as {{"{{participant.fullName}}"}}, {{"{{event.name}}"}} or an answer, {{"{{answers.food}}"}}.
  4. The preview on the right is drawn by the same code as the real pass. Switch the sample between a short name, a very long one, a non-Latin one and a cancelled pass.
  5. Save makes a new version. Older versions stay as they were.

Use your own artwork

If you designed the pass in another tool, such as Canva, Figma or Photoshop, you can use that design.

  1. Export it as PNG or JPEG: 1080 × 1920 px (portrait) for a phone pass, or 1200 × 900 px (landscape) for a printed badge. Leave a plain area for the QR code, about half the pass's width on a phone. Leave out the name and the code: they're different for each person.
  2. On Pass templates, under Upload your own design, give it a name, choose the file and Upload and place.
  3. The designer opens with your artwork filling the pass and the name, QR code and pass ID on white panels. Move them where your design has room; delete the panels if the background there is already plain and light.
  4. Save, then pick it on the event's Pass tab.

A cancelled, waitlisted or pending pass shows its status in red above the name, so staff can tell at a glance. To add a logo or sponsor strip to any design, use + Image in the designer.

Uploaded images are re-saved before they're stored, which removes hidden details such as camera location. They're only used on your organization's passes.

Scan checks

A design can't be saved if its QR code might not scan. The designer lists any problem as you work: the code must be dark on light with strong contrast, keep a clear margin, be large enough on a phone and in print, and have nothing on top of it. Every sample name must fit.

Use it for an event

On the event's Pass tab, choose the design and Use this design. New passes use it. Passes already sent keep the old look until you choose Re-send passes; their QR codes stay the same, so old and new both scan.

Saving a new version of a template doesn't change events that use it until someone picks the new version on their Pass tab.
