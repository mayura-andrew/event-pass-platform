Events are shared like documents: add people by email on the event's Share tab and choose a role.

RoleCan
OwnerEverything, including moving the event to Trash and handing ownership to someone else.
EditorChange the event, its questions, pass, checkpoints and workshops; manage participants; share; scan and undo any scan.
Check-in staffScan passes and look people up, and undo their own scans within 30 seconds. They don't see the participant list or the dashboard.
ViewerSee the event, its participants and dashboard. No changes.

Organization owners and admins can open every event in the organization. People with an account get access at once; anyone else gets an email invite to accept.

Link sharing

Under Link sharing you can turn on a view-only link for people without an account. The link is shown once; reset it to get a new one, or turn it off. Check-in access is never given by link.

Your organization can turn off sharing outside the organization in Members.
