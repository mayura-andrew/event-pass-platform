Connect a Google account and the event gets its own spreadsheet with three tabs, Participants, Check-ins and Workshops, kept up to date within about a minute of any change.

  1. On the event's Integrations tab, choose Connect Google Sheets.
  2. Sign in to Google and allow access. The app can only create and edit the spreadsheets it makes, not the rest of your Drive.
  3. The spreadsheet is created at once. Use Open the spreadsheet, and share it in Google like any other sheet.

Good to know

  • It's one way. The app rewrites the tabs after each change, so edits made in the sheet are lost. Add your own tabs for notes and formulas; the app leaves other tabs alone.
  • Sync now updates it immediately.
  • If the tab says Needs reconnecting, Google access was removed (or the password changed). Choose Connect again.
  • Disconnect stops updates. The spreadsheet stays in Google Drive.
