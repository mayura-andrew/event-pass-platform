When you register for an event, your pass arrives by email. You can also find it, and every other pass you have, under My passes.

Find your pass

  1. Go to My passes and sign in with the email address you registered with. We'll email you a sign-in link; no password needed.
  2. Choose the event to open your pass.

On the day

  • Show the QR code on your phone, with the screen brightness turned up. Or download the image, or print the PDF at actual size (100%).
  • The same pass works at every checkpoint and workshop of the event.
  • The pass page lets you sign up for workshops, or leave one, while there are places.

Your profile and the participant map Beta

On your pass page, Your profile lets you add a photo, a one-line headline and links to LinkedIn, GitHub or your website.

  • It starts as Only me. Choose Other participants of this event to let them see it. It's never shown outside the event.
  • If the organizers have turned on the participant map, Find participants on the map shows who's there. You appear on the map only while you press Share my location and keep the page open. Stop sharing, or leaving the page, removes you at once; if your connection drops, you disappear within 3 minutes.
  • Your photo is re-saved before it's stored, which removes hidden details such as where it was taken.

Tell the organizers what you think with the 👍 / 👎 at the bottom of the map.

Can't find the email? Check spam, then sign in to My passes. Registered with a different address? Ask the event's organizer.
