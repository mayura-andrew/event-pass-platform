On the day, staff scan passes at checkpoints, such as Check in, Kit and lunch. The dashboard shows it all live.

Before the doors open

  1. On the Check-in tab, look over the checkpoints. Every event starts with Confirm participation, Check in and Kit; add your own with Add checkpoint.
  2. For each checkpoint choose whether it can be recorded more than once per person (re-entry, meals), what must be recorded first (Kit after Check in), who can record it, and the hours it's open.
  3. Share the event with door staff as Check-in staff, and have each open Scan on their phone once to allow the camera. Scanner tips.
  4. Do a test scan with your own pass.

What the colors mean

  • Green: recorded, or ready to record.
  • Amber: look before letting them through. Already recorded (with the time and who scanned), not signed up for this workshop, or registration not confirmed.
  • Red: stop. Cancelled, a pass for another event, not a valid pass, outside the checkpoint's hours, or your role can't record this checkpoint.

Two phones scanning the same person at the same moment can't both record a once-only checkpoint: one gets green, the other amber.

Mistakes and lost passes

  • Undo takes a scan back. Check-in staff can undo their own scans for 30 seconds; editors and owners can undo any.
  • No pass? Use Find someone on the scanner to search by name, email or pass ID, then record from there.

Watching it live

The Dashboard tab updates as scans happen: counts per checkpoint, check-ins per 10 minutes, workshop attendance and the latest scans. A connected Google Sheet follows within a minute.
