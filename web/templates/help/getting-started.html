An event goes from idea to scanned pass in six steps. New organizations see them as a checklist on the events home, ticked off as you go.

  1. Create an event. On your organization's events home, choose New event. Give it a name, dates, time zone and venue. You can change all of it later under Settings.
  2. Add registration questions. On the event's Registration tab, add what you need to know beyond name and email: food preference, T-shirt size, team. More on registration.
  3. Design the pass. Every event starts with the Classic pass. To change it, open Pass templates, start from a gallery design, then pick it on the event's Pass tab. More on pass design.
  4. Publish. On the Registration tab, choose Publish. A public or unlisted event gets a registration page; each person who registers gets a pass by email.
  5. Share with check-in staff. On the Share tab, add the people working the door with the Check-in staff role. They can scan, and nothing else. More on roles.
  6. Do a test scan. Register yourself on the public page, open Scan on a phone and scan your own pass. More on the scanner.

Already have a list of people in a Google Form? Import it instead of asking them to register again.
