Participant map Beta

Participants can add a photo, headline and links to their pass page and make them visible to each other. With the map on, registered participants can also see each other on a live map, if they choose to share their location.

  • Profiles are private until each person makes theirs public, and public means this event's participants only.
  • Nobody is on the map unless they press Share my location. It stops when they leave the page, and a location disappears after 3 minutes without an update.
  • Only registered participants see the map; pending, waitlisted and cancelled people don't.
  • Turning the map off deletes every shared location at once.

Under-18 participants? Check this fits your safeguarding policy before turning it on.
